About Us

Queens of Change Foundation is a women-led NGO dedicated to transforming the lives of underprivileged women and girls across India. We believe that menstrual health should never be a barrier to dignity, education, or opportunity. Founded on the principle that every woman deserves respect and support, we work directly in communities to provide sanitary pads, health education, and empowerment opportunities. We are 100% women-led and driven by the lived experiences of the communities we serve. Our approach is holistic—we don't just distribute products, we transform beliefs, break stigma, and create pathways to independence for women and girls.
